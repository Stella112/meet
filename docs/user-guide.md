# Try Meet: one real payment

Real money on Celo mainnet, in small amounts. Meet is a hackathon prototype, not a licensed money service.

**You need:** a Celo wallet you already use, with about $1.10 of USD₮ plus a little for gas, and a wallet address to pay. It can be a friend's, a landlord's, or a second wallet of yours.

1. Check your wallet at https://usemeet.website/try
2. Open https://usemeet.website/app in your wallet's browser, or a desktop browser with your wallet. Choose your Celo wallet.
3. Pick **Bills** or **Payroll**. Enter the payee's wallet, **₦1,400**, and a due date tomorrow or later.
4. Check the worst-case amount, then approve it and sign. Signing does not move your money.
5. Meet pays before the due date. Your calendar shows the receipt and a link to the transaction.

You can cancel any time before it pays. If it can't pay, you'll see "Not paid, no funds moved."

Please tell the builder if anything confuses you or breaks.
